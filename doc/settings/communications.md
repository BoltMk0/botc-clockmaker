# Settings → Communications

[← Settings overview](README.md) · [Documentation home](../README.md)

What the grimoire's [communications](../grimoire.md#communications-showing-things-to-players) offer, in two tabs:
**Presets**, ready-made messages, and **Texts**, the lines of text to build messages from. Each tab has its own
**Save changes**; switching tabs keeps what you haven't saved yet.

## Presets

Ready-made messages, listed in the grimoire's [Preset tab](../grimoire.md#preset) in every game, in the order listed
here, to show in one tap. It starts with *"This is your demon"*, *"These are your minions"* and *"Speak to me tomorrow"*.

Each preset has a **name** — its button in the grimoire — and a message, shown below the name as it'll look.

- **+ Add preset** starts a new one in the message builder. **Edit** opens one there.
- The builder works as in the grimoire's [Custom tab](../grimoire.md#custom): **Add element**, then **Text**,
  **Character** or **Alignment**. With no script here, the characters are every character, by type. **Done** (or
  **Back**) returns to the list.
- **↑** / **↓** move a preset up or down the list; **✕** deletes it.

Press **Save changes** when you're done.

You can also save a preset from the grimoire: build a message in the Custom tab and tick **Save as a preset** before
**Show**.

## Texts

The lines of text offered by the message builder in the grimoire's [Custom tab](../grimoire.md#custom) (and in the
Presets tab here) in every game, in the order listed here. It starts with the usual ones — *"This is your demon"*, *"These are your
minions"*, *"You are"*, *"…and is this player…"* and so on — for you to change, reorder or delete.

Each text has a **style**:

- **Header** — large, in the title font;
- **Text** — smaller, in the normal font.

The communications builder shows each text in its style, and so does the message players see.

- Choose the style from the dropdown and type the text beside it. Line breaks are kept, and words between asterisks
  are bold: `You are *drunk*` shows *"You are **drunk**"*.
- **↑** / **↓** move a text up or down the list; **✕** deletes it.
- **+ Add text** adds a new one.

Press **Save changes** when you're done. Blank texts are dropped when you save.

You can also add a text from the grimoire: in the builder, **Text → Custom…**, and tick **Save as a text option**
before adding it. It's added to the end of this list.
