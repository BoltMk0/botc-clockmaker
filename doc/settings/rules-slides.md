# Settings → Rules Slides

[← Settings overview](README.md) · [Documentation home](../README.md)

The images shown, in order, by the [rules slideshow](../player-pages.md#the-rules-slideshow) — for example a "how to
play" walkthrough, the night order, or house rules.

- **Add** images with the upload button, or **drop image files** onto the page. PNG, JPEG, WebP and GIF are
  accepted, and several can be added at once. Landscape images sized for your display (e.g. 1920×1080) look best.
- **Drag** a slide to move it. Drop it outside the slides (or press Escape) to cancel.
- Click a slide's image to open it full size.
- The **bin** deletes a slide.
- **View slideshow** opens the slideshow.

Changes are saved as you make them. Once there's at least one slide, a **Rules** button appears on the home screen.
