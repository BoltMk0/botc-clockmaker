# Finding your way around

A map of Clockmaker's pages, and what each one is for.

## Play

Your list of games. Each game's card shows its script, player count and day, and has:

- **Town Square** — the big-screen display for the players;
- **Grimoire** — the virtual grimoire, for the storyteller's tablet;
- the **remote** button (top right) — the clock remote, for the storyteller's phone;
- **Clock SFX** — which bell sounds the game plays;
- the **delete** button (top right).

## Town Square

What the players see: the clock, the day, the player count and, with a grimoire, the seated players. Open the ☰ menu
(top left) to change how it looks on that screen:

- **Display Mode** — the 3D clocktower, or simpler displays for slower devices;
- **Display Size** and the now-playing banner;
- **Script QR** and your custom QR codes, shown on the display;
- **Share QR Code** — a QR code for this page, to get another device onto it quickly;
- **Audio Engine** — whether this screen plays sound;
- **Open Clock Remote** — opens the remote on this device.

These options are remembered by each screen separately, so the TV and a tablet can look different.

## Clock remote

The storyteller's controls for one game: the phase (morning, day, dusk, night), the countdown, adding or taking off
time, and the day and player count. It also shows what music is playing, with a skip button.

## Grimoire

The virtual grimoire: the town circle of player tokens, reminder tokens, the night order, the clock and
**communications** (messages and characters to show to players). Its sidebar holds setup, the night order, the
token tray, drawing tools, undo and **End game**.

## Rules

A full-screen slideshow of the rules, for a second screen while players arrive. The button only appears once rules
slides have been uploaded in Settings.

## Settings

Everything shared between all games and devices:

| Page | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| **Characters** | The character library: names, abilities, tokens and reminders. |
| **Scripts** | Scripts, their night order, and saved setups (presets). |
| **Timer Options** | The day lengths offered when you start the day. |
| **Communications** | Ready-made messages the grimoire can show to players. |
| **QR Codes** | Extra QR codes to show on the town square. |
| **Audio** | Bell sounds, ambience and stings. |
| **Music** | Music to play during the game. |
| **Rules Slides** | Images for the rules slideshow. |
| **Feedback** | What players have sent through the feedback form. |
| **Password** | Change the password, or sign every device out. |
| **API Token** | Lets other programs (such as smart lighting) connect. |

## Pages for players

Players don't need the password for these, so they can open them on their own phones:

- **The script viewer** — the current script's characters and abilities. Players get to it by scanning the script
  QR code on the town square.
- **The feedback form** — for players to tell you how the game went.
