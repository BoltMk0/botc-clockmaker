# Physical Grim + Companion App

This is by far the simplest way of storytelling, as it's the most familiar to experienced storytellers.

## Requirements
- Physical Grimoire
- Ambience hosts, any combination of:
    - Phone/tablet/laptop for audio playback
    - laptop set up for lightscapes
- Mobile phone or tablet for remote control (or any device with access to the server really)

## Setup
### Part 1: Configuring the app:
1. Open the clockmaker app on any device
2. Click/tap "Play"
3. If there are no games defined:
    - Tap the "New Game" button (in the top-right corner). The new game's card should now appear. 
    - Set the Clock SFX as desired.
    - Enable "Lightscapes lighting"

### Part 2: Mobile remote control
1. Open the clockmaker app on your mobile device
2. Click/tap "Play"
3. Click "Remote" for the game you want to be controlling

### Part 3: Ambience Audio Engine
1. Open the Clockmaker app on your ambience hosts.
2. Click "Play"
3. Click "Autio Only (Mixer)" at the bottom of this page
4. If the Music channel strip doesn't automatically start, click the "+ Music" channel strip to initialize it.
