# Understanding the game phases

The game logic inside the app is divided into 4 phases:

| Phase | Description | Example Ambience |
|--|--|--|
| Morning | Players wake from night to learn who died | Music ducks or pauses, lighting takes on a warm white hue |
| Day | The timer is set and players go off for private conversations. | Music and ambience is louder, and lighting follows the sun's natural light |
| Dusk | The timer ends and players return to the town square for public conversation and nominations | Music ducks or pauses again, and the lighting goes red |
| Night | Players close their eyes for the night | Music & ambience grows loud, blue lighting |
