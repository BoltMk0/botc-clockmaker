Bells

Lights to flash when a bell rings, held for a moment before going back to the phase's.

Colours that lights can follow in a phase, picked as the light's colour. They fade through them as the day's countdown runs, like the town square's sky: from dawn at the start to the night sky as it runs out. In the morning they show the first colour, and at dusk and night the last.
